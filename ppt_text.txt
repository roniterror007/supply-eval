Slide 6:
7. The Killer Feature: Planning Digital Twin
Change one assumption and propagate its impact through the complete planning chain.
Example: Demand +20% → factory overload → production bottleneck → material shortage → customer delivery risk.
Compare alternatives: shift factory, overtime, expedite supplier, pre-build inventory or prioritize customers.
Rank scenarios by revenue protection, cost, service level and operational risk.

User asks
“What happens if November demand increases by 20%?”

Agent answers
“Factory A reaches 108% utilization and Material M1 has a 600-unit gap. Shift $2M to Factory B + expedite M1.”

Slide 11:
12. Human-in-the-Loop & Enterprise Guardrails

Read / Analyze
Agents can inspect authorized data and run approved models.

Recommend
Agents produce evidence-backed actions, confidence and expected impact.

Approve
Material ERP / procurement / production changes require human approval.
Role-based and business-line access
Grounded responses with source / data references
Structured tool calls and validation
Approval thresholds for high-value actions
Full audit trail of agent reasoning, tools, inputs, outputs and decisions
Prompt-injection and data-exfiltration protections
